### **OIR SET 45 (Non-Verbal / 40 Questions)**

**Category: Figure Matrix (Class A vs Class B)**
**1.** Which of the following figures belongs to **Class A**?  
*Class A: Shapes with an even number of sides.* *Class B: Shapes with an odd number of sides.* (1) Triangle (2) Pentagon (3) Hexagon (4) Septagon

**2.** Which of the following figures belongs to **Class A**?  
*Class A: Figures with two internal dots.* *Class B: Figures with one internal dot.* (1) Square with 2 dots (2) Circle with 1 dot (3) Triangle with 1 dot (4) Hexagon with 3 dots

**3.** Which of the following figures belongs to **Class A**?  
*Class A: Curved closed figures.* *Class B: Straight-line closed figures.* (1) Square (2) Rhombus (3) Ellipse (4) Trapezium

**4.** Which of the following figures belongs to **Class A**?  
*Class A: Arrows pointing Clockwise.* *Class B: Arrows pointing Anti-clockwise.* (1) ↻ (2) ↺ (3) ↑ (4) ↓

**Category: Cube Comparison (Spatial Reasoning)**
**5.** Find if the two cubes are alike. Write **'Yes'** or **'No'**:  
*Cube 1: Front: A, Top: B, Right: C* *Cube 2: Front: B, Top: A, Right: C* 

**6.** Find if the two cubes are alike. Write **'Yes'** or **'No'**:  
*Cube 1: Front: 5, Top: 2, Right: 1* *Cube 2: Front: 1, Top: 2, Right: 6 (Note: Standard dice)*

**7.** Find if the two cubes are alike. Write **'Yes'** or **'No'**:

**8.** Find if the two cubes are alike. Write **'Yes'** or **'No'**:

**Category: Figure Series Completion**
