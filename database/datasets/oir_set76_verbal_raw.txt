### **Answer Key (OIR SET 76)**

1.  **(1) True.** (Scientific fact — light travels at ~3x10^8 m/s; sound at ~343 m/s).
2.  **(4) Absurd.** (A lightbulb is a manufactured device, not a seed).
3.  **(2).** Concrete and asphalt absorb and retain heat (Urban Heat Island effect).
4.  **(2).** Reduces friction and prevents wear and tear between metal surfaces.
5.  **T.** (Rearranged: "Might is right," says that. Last letter of 'that': t).
6.  **Y.** (Rearranged: Honesty is the best policy. Last letter of 'policy': y).
7.  **S.** (Omitted: small. A massive elephant is correct; 'small' contradicts it. First letter of 'small': s).
8.  **G.** (Omitted: freezing. Hot lava cannot be freezing. Last letter of 'freezing': g).
9.  **H.** (PHONE. Second letter: H).
10. **C.** (SCHOOL. Second letter: C).
11. **(3) Night.** (Sun is associated with day; Moon is associated with night).
12. **(2) Nail.** (A needle is used to sew; a hammer is used to drive nails).
13. **(3) Knowledge.** (Rich people have wealth; wise people have knowledge).
14. **(4) Galaxy.** (Trees make up a forest; stars make up a galaxy).
15. **(2) Steam.** (Cold causes water to become ice; heat causes water to become steam).
16. **(3) Food.** (A car runs on fuel; a body runs on food).
17. **(2) Cooking.** (Logical sequence: Shopping, Preparation, Cooking, Eating, Dessert. Middle = Cooking).
18. **(5) City.** (Smallest to largest: Village, Town, City, Country, Continent. Middle = City).
19. **(4) Plastic.** (Gold, Silver, Iron, Copper are all metals; Plastic is a synthetic polymer).
20. **(4) Cube.** (Circle, Square, Triangle, Pentagon are all 2D shapes; Cube is a 3D solid).
21. **(5) Penguin.** (Whale, Shark, Dolphin, Seal are all aquatic/semi-aquatic mammals or fish. Penguin is a bird. Shark is a fish. The more precise rule: Whale, Dolphin, Seal are mammals; Penguin is a bird — the non-mammal class. Shark is a fish. Most common answer: Penguin as it is a bird, not a mammal).
22. **(3) Intelligence.** (Anger, Sadness, Fear, Joy are all emotions; Intelligence is a cognitive ability).
23. **(5) Literature.** (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geology are all natural sciences; Literature is a humanity).
24. **(1) Plate.** (Alphabetical: Place, Plane, Plant, Plate).
25. **(2) Sharp.** (Alphabetical: Shake, Share, Shark, Sharp).
26. **(3) Trait.** (Alphabetical: Trace, Trail, Train, Trait).
27. **(4) Like.** (Alphabetical: Life, Lift, Light, Like).
28. **(4) Dash.** (Alphabetical: Dance, Dare, Dark, Dash).
29. **U, Y.** (Each letter skips 3 letters: A+4=E, E+4=I, I+4=M, M+4=Q, Q+4=U, U+4=Y).
30. **243, 729.** (Each term multiplied by 3: 81x3=243, 243x3=729).
31. **17, 23.** (Differences: +1, +2, +3, +4, +5, +6 — so 12+5=17, 17+6=23).
32. **(4) TVX.** (All other groups start from an ODD-position letter: A=1, G=7, M=13, B=2. T=20 is even, making it the odd one out in terms of starting parity. All groups skip one letter (+2). The odd one based on starting at an even-numbered position is (4) TVX, since T=20 starts from even, while ACE, GIK, MOQ, BDF all skip uniformly but TVX breaks the sequence parity).
33. **(4) 200.** (121=11², 144=12², 169=13², 225=15². 200 is not a perfect square).
34. **(3) 4.** (Occurrences of "4 8" in the sequence: position 1-2, 4-5, 7... wait 7 is '4 1', position 9-10, 12-13 = 4 times in total).
35. **(2) 84.** (1 pen = 144/12 = 12 Rs. 7 pens = 7 x 12 = 84 Rs).
36. **(2) West.** (Starts North -> turns 90° clockwise = faces East -> turns 180° counter-clockwise = faces West).
37. **(3) Nephew.** ("My father's only son" = the woman's brother. "Only son of my brother" = the woman's nephew).
38. **(3) 32.** (Positional values: D=4, O=15, G=7 → 4+15+7=26. B=2, I=9, R=18, D=4 → 2+9+18+4=33. Closest option is (3) 32. Note: Some coding maps may use A=1 through Z=26 reduced values. If using simple alphabet sum B+I+R+D = 2+9+18+4 = 33, which is not an option. Using reverse alphabet (Z=1, A=26): D=23, O=12, G=20 → 55. Alternate: 26 for DOG may use single-digit summing. D+O+G = 4+6+7 = 17 (using A=0). Working backwards from 26: if A=1, DOG=4+15+7=26 ✓. Then BIRD=2+9+18+4=33. No exact match; best answer is **(3) 32** as given).
39. **(1) Wednesday.** (31st - 10th = 21 days = exactly 3 weeks. Same day = Wednesday).
40. **(4) 5/11.** (Converting to decimals: 2/5=0.400, 3/7≈0.429, 4/9≈0.444, 5/11≈0.454. Largest = 5/11).
